Our Mission

Sector-7 is dedicated to protecting truth, defending transparency, and providing a secure platform for whistleblowers to share sensitive information anonymously. We believe information should be free, accountable, and shielded from manipulation.

Inspired by platforms like WikiLeaks, our goal is to empower individuals to expose wrongdoing without fear of retaliation, while ensuring that sensitive submissions are handled responsibly and securely.

Our Values

  • Integrity: Upholding honesty and ethical conduct in all our operations.
  • Security: Prioritizing the safety and anonymity of all contributors and sources.
  • Transparency: Promoting openness, accountability, and access to critical information.
  • Accountability: Ensuring institutions and individuals are answerable for their actions.
  • Courage: Supporting whistleblowers and brave individuals who expose wrongdoing.

What We Do

  • Anonymous Submissions: Secure channels for whistleblowers to submit reports, leaks, and documents.
  • Responsible Publication: Careful review and controlled release of sensitive information for public benefit.
  • Global Protection: Decentralized infrastructure ensures submissions remain accessible and secure from censorship.
  • Collaboration: We work with NGOs, journalists, and media organizations to amplify critical information safely.

Why We Exist

In a world where secrecy is power, Sector-7 provides a counterbalance. Governments, corporations, and intelligence agencies often operate in the shadows. We ensure that citizens have access to information that affects their lives and society.

We combine inspiration from whistleblowing pioneers with modern encryption, anonymous networks like Tor, and best practices in digital security. Our systems allow whistleblowers to safely share sensitive data while protecting their identities.

Important Note for Whistleblowers

Sector-7 prioritizes your anonymity and security. Only submit information through our official channels. Do not include personal identifiers unless absolutely necessary. For safe submission instructions, visit our Reports page.

Our Inspiration

We are inspired by the legacy of WikiLeaks and other transparency movements. These organizations showed that courageous individuals could hold institutions accountable and expose critical information to the public. Sector-7 continues this mission using modern technology, decentralized systems, and enhanced security for contributors worldwide.
